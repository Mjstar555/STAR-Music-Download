## 🌟 What is STAR Music?

**STAR Music** is a multi-platform, distributed audio client engineered for music enthusiasts, audiophiles, and everyday listeners who want a clean, ad-free, and high-performance listening experience.

Unlike conventional music platforms that trap users behind recurring monthly subscriptions, disruptive video ads, mandatory accounts, and DRM-locked downloads, STAR Music provides:
* **Uncompressed 320kbps audio playback** with instant streaming response.
* **True offline caching** stored directly on your hardware that never expires.
* **Real-time multi-device synchronization (<300ms micro-sync)** allowing you and your friends to listen together in perfect harmony.
* **A floating Glassmorphic Mini Player** that stays on top of your games, code editors, and workflow without getting in the way.
* **Hardware-locked zero-login security**, giving you total privacy without email, phone, or password requirements.

## 🚀 Complete Feature Walkthrough

### 1. 🔒 Zero-Login Ecosystem & Hardware Enclave
* **What it does:** Eliminates passwords, verification emails, and database breaches.
* **How it works:** When you launch STAR Music, your device generates a unique 256-bit cryptographic hardware signature. VIP access is verified directly against your hardware enclave.
* **Two Device Roles:**
  * **👑 Host Device:** Unlocks the app using your VIP Access Key. The Host owns the library, creates custom playlists, and can host shared listening parties.
  * **🔗 Guest Device:** Connects seamlessly to a Host via an 8-character pairing code or QR code. Guests require **no VIP key** and can listen along without spending a dime.

---

### 2. 🎨 Glassmorphic Desktop Mini Player & Web PiP
* **What it does:** Allows you to control your music from a sleek, transparent, floating widget without keeping the main application window open.
* **Desktop Features (Windows .exe):**
  * **True Acrylic Glassmorphism:** Semi-transparent background with frosted blur that blends into your desktop wallpaper.
  * **Draggable Anywhere:** Click and drag from any edge to reposition it freely across your monitors.
  * **Hover-Revealed Controls:** Controls stay invisible until you hover your mouse over the widget, revealing play/pause, track skip, volume slider, and track artwork.
  * **Always-On-Top:** Stays above full-screen browsers, games, Discord, or code editors.
* **Web Feature (Browser):**
  * Built-in Picture-in-Picture (PiP) audio canvas window that floats above all your browser tabs.

### 3. 📡 STAR Sync (Real-Time Party Mode & Shared Listening)
* **What it does:** Synchronizes audio playback across multiple devices in real-time, whether your friends are across the room or on the other side of the world.
* **How it works:**
  * **Host Controls:** The host initiates the Party Room, generating a unique Room Code (e.g., `PARTY-XXXXXX`) or a 1-click sharing link. The host controls track selection, queue order, play/pause, and seeking.
  * **Continuous Micro-Sync Engine:** Employs an aggressive 300ms time-drift compensation algorithm. If any listener's connection stutters, their player automatically snaps back to within milliseconds of the host's exact audio timestamp.
  * **Guest Non-Revocation:** If a host resets hardware bindings, guest sessions remain active without abrupt disconnects.
  * **Interactive Party Chat & Animated Emojis:** Send real-time chat messages and animated emoji bursts that float on screen during the song. Built-in anti-spam cooldown prevents disruption.

### 4. 📴 Offline Music Engine (True 320kbps Downloads)

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: Is STAR Music really 100% free with no ads?
> **Yes.** STAR Music has zero commercial advertisements, zero audio breaks, and zero subscription fees. It is built as an open, high-fidelity audio client.

### Q: Do I need to create an account or provide my email?
> **No.** STAR Music utilizes a **Zero-Login Architecture**. Your identity and library are cryptographically locked to your hardware signature and VIP key. No emails, phone numbers, or passwords are ever stored.

### Q: How does Offline Music work without a subscription?
> Unlike Spotify (which locks downloaded music into proprietary encrypted cache files that delete themselves if you don't connect to the internet every 30 days), STAR Music downloads the real high-bitrate audio stream and stores it directly into your device's persistent IndexedDB storage. It stays on your device permanently until you choose to delete it.

### Q: Can my friends listen along with me if they don't have a key?
> **Yes!** That is the power of **STAR Sync**. Only the Host needs a VIP setup. The host simply shares their 8-character Room Code or QR code, and friends can join as **Guests** on their phone, laptop, or browser completely free.

### Q: How do I open the Glassmorphic Mini Player?
> On the Windows Desktop app, click the **Mini Player** icon on the bottom player bar (next to the volume slider). The main window hides and the transparent floating player appears on your screen. You can drag it anywhere and control playback with hover gestures.

### Q: Is my Spotify playlist safe when importing?
> **Yes.** STAR Music only reads the public track titles and artist names from the Spotify share link. It does not ask for your Spotify account credentials and cannot modify your Spotify account in any way.
